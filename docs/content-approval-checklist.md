## 1. Executive Summary & Integrity Principles

This document establishes the editorial integrity guidelines and clinical claims verification protocol for the official digital presence of **Samarth Hospital, Jamkhed**. 

Healthcare websites bear ethical and legal responsibility towards patients and their families. In an emergency or acute health condition, inaccurate claims (such as unsupported 24/7 super-specialty claims, unverified doctor degrees, or fake cashless guarantees) can directly compromise patient care and expose the institution to regulatory scrutiny under Maharashtra Medical Council (MMC) and National Medical Commission (NMC) regulations.

### Core Truth & Trust Mandates:
1. **Verified Facts Only:** Only publish facts confirmed through government registers, municipal records, direct hospital documentation, or verified business profiles.
2. **Provisional Labeling:** All unverified clinician qualifications, bed counts, sub-specialty surgeries, and diagnostic equipment must be marked as `[Provisional - Pending Hospital Approval]`.
3. **No Superlatives:** Absolute prohibitions on superlative marketing words ("Best hospital in Maharashtra", "#1 Trauma Care", "100% cure rate", "Top surgeon").
4. **No Fabricated Patient Testimonials:** Under NMC ethics regulations, patient testimonials cannot be invented, paid for, or embellished.
5. **No Medical Advice via Website:** The website serves for institutional information and appointment enquiry routing, explicitly disclaiming emergency medical advice online.
6. **Bilingual Accessibility:** Content must be formulated in high-clarity English and natural, polite Marathi (मराठी), respecting local rural and semi-urban patients from Jamkhed taluka and neighboring Beed/Ashti/Karjat regions.

## 4. Prohibited Content & Compliance Guardrails

1. **NO Fabricated Accreditations:** Do not place NABH, NABL, or JCI logos unless the hospital management supplies verified accreditation certificates.
2. **NO Exaggerated Bed Counts:** Bed counts must remain unstated or listed generally as "well-equipped inpatient wards and ICU" until exact ward numbers are confirmed.
3. **NO Fake Emergency Response Guarantees:** Do not promise "5-minute ambulance arrival" or "zero waiting time".
4. **NO Patient Images Without Release:** Patient photos must be standard illustrative stock assets with clear captions, or authentic photos provided with signed patient consent.
5. **No Direct Drug Recommendations:** Content will never suggest medications, dosages, or self-diagnosis.
