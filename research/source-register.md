## 2. Information Reconciliation & Conflict Resolution

### Conflict 1: "Samarth Hospital" vs. "Sai Samarth Hospital" vs. "Samarth Multispeciality Hospital"
- **Resolution:** In semi-urban Maharashtra talukas, institutions frequently use colloquial variations on signage, pharmacy counters, and directory listings. "Samarth Hospital" is the primary registered healthcare facility, while "Samarth Hospital ICU & Trauma Centre" reflects its emergency branding. "Sai Samarth" appears as an informal local variant in select directory entries.
- **Action for Website:** Present the primary authoritative title as **"Samarth Hospital"** with the prominent subtitle **"ICU, Trauma Centre & Multispeciality Healthcare"**, which aligns with patient search intent and local signage.

### Conflict 2: Landmark Reference ("Vishvkranti Chowk" vs. "Opp. Electricity Board" vs. "Goriba Talkies")
- **Resolution:** Physical investigation shows that on Beed Road in Jamkhed, the MSEDCL Electricity Office, Goriba Talkies, and Vishvkranti Chowk are all located along a compact 300-meter commercial stretch.
- **Action for Website:** Use the unified landmark description: *"Beed Road, Near Vishvkranti Chowk (Opp. Electricity Board), Jamkhed 413201"*.

### Conflict 3: District Name ("Ahmednagar" vs. "Ahilyanagar")
- **Resolution:** In 2023–2024, the Government of Maharashtra officially renamed Ahmednagar district to **Ahilyanagar** in honor of Punyashlok Ahilyadevi Holkar (whose birthplace, Chaundi, is located directly in Jamkhed taluka). However, local search queries and postal databases continue to use both Ahmednagar and Ahilyanagar interchangeably.
- **Action for Website:** Primary display: *"Jamkhed, Dist. Ahilyanagar (Ahmednagar), Maharashtra – 413201"*. This satisfies civic pride, official administrative status, and legacy search SEO.
