## 5. Clinical Departments, Infrastructure & Diagnostic Capabilities

### A. Critical Care & Trauma Services
* **Intensive Care Unit (ICU):** Dedicated critical care unit equipped with multi-para patient monitors, oxygen support, critical life support infrastructure, and round-the-clock nursing supervision.
* **Accident & Highway Trauma Centre:** Immediate resuscitation, wound management, polytrauma care, fracture stabilization, and acute medical stabilization along the high-incident Beed-Jamkhed corridor.
* **Emergency Casualty Care:** 24/7 triage, poisoning/snakebite emergency management, acute pain, cardiovascular/respiratory initial stabilization.

### B. Surgical & Orthopedic Facilities
* **Orthopedics & Fracture Management:** Comprehensive bone, joint, and trauma injury treatment.
* **Advanced C-Arm Fluoroscopy:** On-site surgical C-arm machine enabling high-precision, real-time fluoroscopic imaging for orthopedic fixation and minimally invasive surgical interventions.
* **Surgical Suite / Operation Theatre:** Clean operating setup for trauma, general, and orthopedic surgical procedures.

### C. Diagnostics & Ancillary Infrastructure
* **Digital X-Ray / Radiography:** Fast digital imaging for skeletal trauma, chest, and emergency diagnostics.
* **Affiliated MRI Centre:** Associated imaging diagnostic facility via Sai Samarth setup providing high-resolution scan access to rural patients without traveling to major district hubs.
* **Pathology / Laboratory Support:** Routine and emergency diagnostic blood, urine, and biochemical tests.
* **Pharmacy:** In-house / adjacent 24/7 dispensing of essential emergency medications, surgical consumables, and IV fluids.
* **Ambulance Coordination:** Patient transport and emergency transfer capabilities.

### D. Community & Rural Outreach
* Regular organizer of free health checkup camps, bone density and orthopedic screenings, and public health awareness drives in Jamkhed taluka.
