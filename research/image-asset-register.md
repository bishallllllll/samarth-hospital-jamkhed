## 1. Visual Integrity & Truth-in-Advertising Rules

1. **Explicit Illustrative Labeling:** Under Medical Council ethics and consumer protection standards, generic stock photography must **never** be presented as actual interior rooms, medical staff, or equipment of Samarth Hospital. Every stock asset in the prototype is tracked in this register and labeled as *"Representative Clinical Imagery"* in metadata and UI.
2. **No Exterior Impersonation:** No external photo of a third-party hospital building is presented as the Samarth Hospital facade. The website uses clean typographic branding, graphic badges, and interior medical illustrations until authentic exterior photos are taken.
3. **No Fabricated Patient Photography:** No stock photos are paired with fabricated patient recovery quotes or fake testimonials.
4. **Legally Usable Commercial Licenses:** All prototype visual assets are sourced under the **Unsplash Commercial License** (free to use, modify, and display commercially without royalty) or public domain archives.
5. **Hospital Onboarding Deliverable:** This register includes a specific "Photo Request Checklist" to guide Dr. Bharat Darkunde and his administrative team in capturing authentic, high-resolution photos of their Jamkhed facility prior to production deployment.

## 3. Checklist of Authentic Photography to Request from Hospital Management

During the client demonstration to Dr. Bharat Darkunde and the hospital management, provide this structured checklist so their team can supply authentic photographs for final production deployment:

### Essential Facade & Exterior (Day & Night)
- [ ] **Hospital Exterior & Building Facade:** Full front view showing main signboard (*Samarth Hospital / ICU & Trauma Centre*).
- [ ] **Main Entrance & Ramp:** Showing ambulance bay, wheelchair accessibility ramp, and emergency casualty entrance.
- [ ] **Highway Approach / Road Signage:** View from Beed Road looking towards the hospital entrance near Vishvkranti Chowk.

### Clinical & Critical Care Areas
- [ ] **Intensive Care Unit (ICU):** Wide shot showing patient beds, cardiac monitors, ventilator units, and central nursing station.
- [ ] **Operation Theatre (OT):** Sterile view showing surgical table, shadowless lamps, anaesthesia trolley, and autoclave room.
- [ ] **Casualty / Emergency Bay:** Immediate examination stretchers, oxygen ports, and emergency resuscitation kit.
- [ ] **Inpatient Wards & Private Rooms:** General male/female wards, semi-private rooms, and deluxe single rooms showing beds and clean bathrooms.
- [ ] **In-House Pharmacy:** Counter, medicine storage racks, and 24/7 billing window.
- [ ] **Laboratory & Diagnostics:** Diagnostic equipment, X-ray room, or ECG room.

### People & Leadership
- [ ] **Dr. Bharat Darkunde:** High-resolution professional portrait in clinical attire (white coat / stethoscope).
- [ ] **Dr. Sonali Bharat Darkunde:** High-resolution professional portrait in clinical attire.
- [ ] **Clinical & Nursing Team:** Group photograph of nursing staff, receptionists, and ward assistants in uniform at the front desk.

### Amenities & Infrastructure
- [ ] **Reception & Registration Desk:** Clean, welcoming reception area with patient registration counter.
- [ ] **Patient Waiting Lounge:** Shaded outdoor or indoor seating for family attendants.
- [ ] **Generator & Power Backup Unit:** Showing uninterrupted power infrastructure.
- [ ] **Ambulance:** Hospital ambulance or tie-up vehicle showing emergency contact markings.
